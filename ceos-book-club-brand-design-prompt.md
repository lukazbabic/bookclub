# CEO's Book Club: Brand & Design Prompt
*A complete brief for building brand guidelines. Hand this to a designer or paste into an AI design tool.*

---

## 1. Brand Foundation

**Name:** CEO's Book Club
**Origin:** Founded at FEFA Faculty (Psychology & Business), Belgrade, as a place to stay accountable to read more, and to *keep* what you read by discussing it. Piloted for a year with a core group; now open to all students in Belgrade, including internationals. English-language books and discussions, biweekly meetings at FEFA.

**Guiding quote (the brand's north star):**
> "Your success in life will be determined largely by your ability to speak, your ability to write, and the quality of your ideas."
> Patrick Winston, MIT

This quote *is* the club's operating system, and the format maps to it one-to-one: **speak** (biweekly discussions), **write** (the follow-up notes after every session), **ideas** (the books). Use it prominently: on the website, at the first session of each season, in the guidelines.

**Positioning statement:**
The book club for students who read like future leaders. We read the books that shape CEOs (business, psychology, science) and the classics everyone pretends to have read. Then we argue about them and write down what stuck. In English, in person, in Belgrade.

**Core idea (the creative concept):**
**"Leadership is a draft."** Nobody arrives finished. Like a crosshatched drawing, understanding builds line by line, layer by layer, meeting by meeting. Unlike most reading, it's designed to be retained: spoken, written, kept. The brand celebrates visible process: messy sketchbook pages, not polished portraits. This is the conceptual bridge between the CEO ambition and the sketchbook aesthetic: mastery in progress.

**Signature ritual, "Margin Notes":**
After every session, the club publishes a short written follow-up: the key discussion points and the ideas the group took with them. This ran through the whole pilot year and carries into every season. It is a brand asset, not admin. It is the proof that the club retains what it reads, and the "write" leg of the Winston quote. It gets its own designed template (see §3).

**Values:** ideas you keep, not just read · discussion over lecture · accountability without pressure · showing your work · ambition without pretension

**Audience:** university students in Belgrade (18–26), Serbian and international, business/psychology/econ-leaning but open to all faculties. English-comfortable, ambitious, tired of passive lectures.

**Personality:** a sharp, well-read friend who takes ideas seriously and themselves lightly. Confident, warm, a little irreverent. Never corporate, never academic, never influencer-polished.

## 2. Voice & Copy

**Voice rules:**
- Short sentences. Concrete verbs. No jargon ("synergy," "unlock," "journey" are banned).
- Self-aware about the ambitious name: lean into it with a wink, never defensively.
- Write like marginalia: direct, personal, slightly unpolished.
- English throughout; occasional Belgrade references welcome.
- Avoid using em dashes, avoid AI-speak, "it's not x. It's y" sentence phrasing, and other stereotypical AI voices.

## 3. Visual Identity: Design Prompt

*This section is the prompt for generating the brand guidelines document and all assets.*

### Style reference: "modern sketchbook, working session"

The brand lives at the intersection of three surfaces:

1. **The sketchbook.** Pen-and-ink **crosshatching** (reference: The Art Coach's crosshatching tutorials on YouTube). All illustration is built from layered hatch lines; nothing is filled, darkness is earned line by line. Signature motif: every key drawing shows its own process: one half fully rendered, one half loose contour lines.
2. **The workshop wall.** The design-thinking layer. Yellow post-it notes carrying single bold words, sticky index tabs as section labels, a dark chalkboard panel for the idea that anchors the room. This is what makes the brand feel *in use*, not archival.
3. **The e-reader.** The Kindle/reMarkable paperwhite sensibility that keeps it modern and calm: matte surfaces, warm paper tone everywhere, no gloss, no gradients on UI surfaces, no shadows except under physical objects (post-its), generous whitespace, restrained modern type. The page should feel like a premium reading device someone sketches and sticks notes on.

The mix in practice: paperwhite calm as the base (70%), ink sketching as the voice (20%), post-it/chalkboard workshop energy as the punctuation (10%). Handwriting appears **only inside drawn artwork** (illustrations, logo lettering, the Margin Notes page), never as a UI font.

### Logo (final assets exist)

Transparent-PNG suite, ink-on-transparent so it sits on any paper tone:
- **Mark:** open book in crosshatch, left page loose contours, right page fully rendered
- **Wordmark:** hand-lettered "CEO's Book Club" with red underline (the handwriting is *drawn*, part of the artwork)
- **Square lockup:** book above wordmark; avatar/favicon/social profile
- Usage: mark + wordmark side by side in headers; square lockup for profiles; always on paper tone, never on photos

### Color

- **Paper (base):** warm sketchbook tan **#EBE0CC**, matched to the illustration paper; used on every page, edge to edge, e-ink matte. Never pure white.
- **Ink:** warm sepia-black **#1E1B16**, never pure black
- **Red (the highlighter):** **#B3402A**, in two registers: opaque red-pen strokes (underlines, step numbers, CTA outlines) and a translucent *highlighter swipe* (red at ~25% opacity over text, like a highlighted passage on an e-reader). The red is the only color allowed to touch words.
- **Post-it yellow:** **#F5DF6B**, the primary workshop accent; carries single bold words, section tabs, prices. Secondary stickies for grouped concepts only: coral **#F2A98F**, sky **#A9CFDE**.
- **Chalkboard:** deep charcoal-green **#25281F** with chalk text **#EFEAD9**; one panel per page maximum
- **Pencil grey:** **#8A8578** for construction lines and asides
- Ratio: paper 70 / ink 18 / yellow 6 / red 4 / chalkboard 2. No gradients on UI, no neon, no corporate blue.

### Typography (no handwriting fonts in UI)

- **Display:** **Space Grotesk** (600–700), a modern grotesque for headlines, buttons, post-it lettering, numbers. Workshop energy comes from weight and placement, not from faux-handwritten faces.
- **Body:** **Lora**, a literary serif, comfortable on-screen reading; italic Lora replaces handwriting for small margin asides
- **Labels/meta:** **IBM Plex Mono** for dates, tags, sticky tabs, page-number-style metadata
- Type sits on the grid; only physical objects (post-its, taped photos) rotate 1–3°

### Illustration system

Commission/generate a set of crosshatched spot illustrations, all in the same ink style:
- Portraits of "thinkers at the table": students mid-argument, hands gesturing, coffee cups (never stock-photo people; always ink)
- Objects: stacked books, a dog-eared page, reading glasses, a chess piece, a chair pulled out
- Each book cycle gets its own crosshatched illustration interpreting the book's core idea; this becomes the announcement asset for web/LinkedIn/Instagram
- Progress motif everywhere: at least one element per composition left as bare contour lines

### Web design direction

- Layout is paperwhite-calm: one warm paper tone edge to edge, generous margins, content column on grid, subtle grain only
- Illustrations are transparent PNGs that blend into the paper (multiply), never boxed, never floating; hero art runs full-bleed and dissolves into the page
- The workshop layer punctuates: one key headline word on a rotated yellow post-it, section kickers as sticky tabs, one chalkboard panel per page (the Winston quote), prices/asides on small stickies
- Red behaves like a pen and a highlighter: animated underline strokes on links, translucent highlight swipes on key passages, red outline CTAs with crosshatch fill on hover
- Section dividers are hand-drawn ink rules, slightly wavy
- Photos of real meetings appear taped in (tape corners, slight rotation), captioned in mono
- Mobile-first; the column translates naturally to phone width

### Motion (optional)

- Line-draw animations: illustrations self-draw on scroll, hatch layers appearing in sequence, literally showing the progress of learning
- Keep durations short (<800ms); the site should feel like paper, not an app

### Deliverables for the brand guidelines document

1. Logo suite (primary lettering, symbol, single-line small version) + clear-space and misuse rules
2. Color palette with hex/print values and usage ratios (paper 70%, ink 25%, red 5%)
3. Type scale for web and print
4. Illustration style guide: 6–8 example spot illustrations, hatching density rules, the "unfinished half" motif explained
5. Templates: Instagram post + story, LinkedIn post image, A4 poster/flyer, website hero
6. **Margin Notes template**, the flagship recurring asset. Designed as a scanned sketchbook page: session number and book title in hand-lettering, key discussion points as handwritten-style margin annotations, the "ideas we took" as short numbered ink notes, a small crosshatched spot illustration of the session's central idea, red-pen underlines on the one line that mattered most. Needs a web/email version and a square social crop. The archive of these pages becomes the club's public body of work: a sketchbook filling up season by season.
7. **Quote treatment**, a definitive lockup of the Patrick Winston quote (hand-lettered, crosshatched portrait of nothing, just the words, with "speak / write / ideas" each underlined in red). Used on the website, season-opening slide, and poster.
8. Do/don't page. Critically: **don't clean it up.** The moment it looks like a polished corporate brand, it's off-brand.

## 4. Image Generation Prompt Pack (ChatGPT)

### How to use

- Every prompt starts with the same **base style block** so the whole set is consistent. Generate the full series in one conversation and reference earlier images ("same style as the previous image") to lock the look.
- Request **transparent background PNG** for every asset except the hero scenes; transparency is what lets images melt into the site's paper instead of floating in boxes.
- Ask for the largest available size; wide assets (hero, banners) in landscape, spots in square.
- Reject any output that looks airbrushed, painterly, or "AI-smooth." The linework must look like a real pen.

### Base style block (prepend to every prompt)

> Hand-drawn pen-and-ink crosshatching illustration, working-sketchbook style: layered hatch lines building tone, visible construction lines and stray marks, sepia-black ink, parts deliberately unfinished as loose contour lines, editorial book-illustration quality, no color except where specified, transparent background PNG.

### The series

**1. Hero: the discussion (wide, ~2400px, paper background allowed)**
> [Base style block] A wide scene of five university students in animated discussion around a round wooden table stacked with books and coffee cups, bookshelves behind them. The two figures on the left fully rendered in dense crosshatch, figures fading progressively to bare contour lines on the right, with the right third of the image almost empty paper. Warm sketchbook paper background #EBE0CC, drawn edge-to-edge with no page borders, no dark vignette at the edges.

**2. About: the glasses (square spot)**
> [Base style block] A pair of round reading glasses lying on a surface, the left lens and frame fully rendered in dense crosshatch with cast shadow, the right lens only loose construction lines, one small red-pen stroke beneath.

**3. Vote: the shortlist (square spot)**
> [Base style block] A stack of five hardcover books, spines at slight angles, top book open; the bottom three books densely crosshatched, the top two in bare outline; a small red ribbon bookmark as the only color.

**4. Discuss: the coffee (square spot)**
> [Base style block] A steaming cup of coffee on a saucer, cup fully rendered in crosshatch, steam drawn as three loose overlapping contour curves, small red-pen underline stroke near the base.

**5. Keep it: Margin Notes page (square or 4:5)**
> [Base style block] A single sketchbook page with clearly legible handwritten notes: heading "MARGIN NOTES / Session 07", three short numbered lines of neat handwriting, one line underlined in red ink, a small crosshatched sketch of an open book in the corner, a yellow post-it note stuck at an angle on the page edge with one word on it in marker: "keep".

**6. Membership: the knight (square spot)**
> [Base style block] A chess knight piece emerging from geometric construction lines and measurement circles, the head and mane fully crosshatched, the base still raw scaffolding lines, one red-pen arc at its base.

**7. Join: the empty table (wide banner, ~2400px)**
> [Base style block] A long wooden table with six chairs, one chair pulled out invitingly toward the viewer, open books and two coffee cups on the table, no people; left side fully rendered, right side dissolving into contour lines; a single yellow post-it stuck on the pulled-out chair's backrest.

**8. Quote lockup: the blackboard (wide, dark background allowed)**
> A dark charcoal chalkboard #25281F drawn edge to edge. On it, in clean white chalk handwriting: "Your success in life will be determined largely by your ability to speak, your ability to write, and the quality of your ideas." Signed: Patrick Winston, MIT. The words "speak", "write", "ideas" underlined in red chalk. Three small yellow post-it notes stuck along the bottom edge of the board reading "SPEAK", "WRITE", "IDEAS" in bold marker capitals. Chalk dust smudges, slightly uneven letters, no other decoration.

**9. Social avatar refresh (square)**
> [Base style block] The open-book logo mark centered, left page loose contours, right page dense crosshatch, above hand-lettered "CEO's Book Club" with a confident red underline stroke; composition fits a circular crop.

**10. Filler spots (set of four small squares, very light)**
> [Base style block] Four separate minimal drawings, mostly bare contour lines with only 20% crosshatch: (a) a bookmark ribbon, (b) a fountain pen, (c) a dog-eared page corner, (d) a sticky tab. Extremely light line density; these are marginalia, not features.

**11. Season poster / LinkedIn banner (wide)**
> [Base style block] A wide composition of a sketchbook lying open next to a small stack of yellow post-it notes and a pen, on warm paper background #EBE0CC; the sketchbook page shows a half-finished crosshatch drawing of an open book; generous empty space on the right half for typography; one red-pen stroke and one yellow post-it as the only color.

### One-line prompt (quick version for any single asset)

> Pen-and-ink crosshatching illustration in a working sketchbook style: [subject], layered hatch lines building tone, one half fully rendered and one half loose contour lines, visible construction marks, sepia-black ink on transparent background, single deep-red accent, optional yellow post-it note, messy but masterful, editorial book-illustration quality
